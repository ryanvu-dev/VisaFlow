# User Journey Map

This User Journey Map outlines how applicants and coordinators currently navigate the visa preparation process, highlighting pain points, emotions, and opportunities for improvement. It focuses on the real-world behaviors and challenges that the product must address.

---

## 1. Applicant Journey

A step-by-step narrative of how applicants experience the visa preparation process today, structured by Actions, Needs and Pains, Customer Feeling, and Opportunities.

| Stage | Actions | Needs and Pains | Customer Feeling | Opportunities | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| **1.1 Trigger Event** | Applicant is told they need a visa to visit Australia. Coordinator explains the general process. | No clear understanding of what is required. Fear of making mistakes. | 😕 Confusion, uncertainty | Clear, simple explanations; early guidance | 
| **1.2 Gathering Documents** | Applicant searches for personal documents (passport, photos, travel history). Sends photos or scans via messaging apps. | Doesn’t know which documents are acceptable. Sends low-quality or incomplete files. Repeated back-and-forth with coordinator. | 😰 Overwhelmed, unsure | Standardize document requirements; improve file quality guidance | 
| **1.3 Providing Information** | Coordinator asks questions (employment, travel history, family details). Applicant answers via chat or voice messages. | Information scattered across messages. Hard to remember what was already sent. Coordinator must repeatedly clarify. | 😤 Frustration, cognitive load | Centralize communication; structured data collection | 
| **1.4 Corrections & Rework** | Coordinator reviews documents and requests corrections. Applicant resends updated files. | Doesn’t understand why corrections are needed. Must redo steps multiple times. No visibility on progress. | 😳 Embarrassment, stress | Enable structured corrections; progress visibility | 
| **1.5 Waiting for Submission** | Applicant waits for coordinator to finish assembling the application. | No visibility on what’s left. Unsure if anything is missing. | 😟 Anxiety, lack of control | Show progress and remaining steps | 
| **1.6 External Authority Stage** | Coordinator submits the visa. Applicant waits for updates. | No clear timeline. Must rely on coordinator for updates. | 😬 Nervousness, hope | Provide shared timeline for external status | 

---

## 2. Coordinator Journey

A narrative of how coordinators manage the process today, structured by Actions, Needs and Pains, Customer Feeling, and Opportunities.

| Stage | Actions | Needs and Pains | Customer Feeling | Opportunities | 
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | 
| **2.1 Initial Explanation** | Coordinator explains visa requirements to applicant. Sends a list of documents and information. | Must repeat explanations for each applicant. Hard to simplify complex requirements. | 😓 Frustration | Reduce repeated explanations; clear documentation | 
| **2.2 Collecting Documents** | Receives photos and files via messaging apps. Saves them manually. | Files arrive in random order. Low-quality images. Hard to track what’s missing. | 😖 Frustration | Standardize document collection; improve file quality | 
| **2.3 Reviewing & Requesting Corrections** | Checks each document. Sends correction notes. Waits for applicant to resend. | Repeated corrections. Applicants forget instructions. No structured review process. | 😩 Frustration | Enable structured review and correction process | 
| **2.4 Assembling the Application** | Manually organizes documents. Fills online forms. Cross-checks details. | Time-consuming. High cognitive load. Risk of missing information. | 😓 Stress | Automate organization; reduce cognitive load | 
| **2.5 Tracking External Status** | Monitors visa authority updates. Notifies applicant. | No unified timeline. Must manually track updates. | 😰 Anxiety | Provide unified timeline and notifications |

## 3. Pain Point Summary

A consolidated view of the most significant issues.

| Applicants                             | Coordinators               |
| -------------------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| Don’t know what to do next.            | Repetitive explanations.   |
| Don’t understand document requirements.| Scattered documents.       |
| Repeated corrections.                  | Manual tracking.           |
| No visibility on progress.             | High cognitive load.       |
| Anxiety during waiting periods.        | Inefficient communication. |

---

## 4. Opportunities for the Product

These opportunities directly inform the MVP and future features.

* Provide a clear, linear workflow.
* Show progress and remaining steps.
* Standardize document requirements.
* Enable structured corrections.
* Centralize communication.
* Reduce repeated explanations.
* Provide a shared timeline for external status.
